You Aren't Too Young To Plan Ahead

5 Things I Wish More 20-Year-Olds Knew About Life Insurance

01

It's About the People You Leave Behind

Life insurance protects the people who rely on you—replacing income, paying debts, and covering final expenses so your family is never left scrambling.

02

You Don't Have to Be Married or Have Kids First

One of the biggest misconceptions is that you need to be older, married, or have children before you even think about coverage. You don't!

03

Younger & Healthier Means Much More Affordable

Getting coverage in your 20s while you're young and healthy locks in far lower monthly rates than waiting until your 30s or 40s.

04

You Don't Need a Huge Income to Start

Even understanding your options for Term Life and a Roth IRA is a huge step in the right direction—and it fits into a normal starter budget.

05

My Job Is Education—Never a High-Pressure Pitch

Asking questions costs $0. I'll walk you through how policies actually work so you can make decisions on your own timeline.
